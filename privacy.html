Privacy Policy

Effective: October 10, 2026

Who we are

Kids PC Time Control is published by Top Shareware for adults managing a shared family computer. This policy covers the Kids PC Time Control family app and its documentation website. Parents should explain the settings to children and use nicknames instead of full names.

Information stored on your PC

The app stores account names and roles, salted password-derived values, failed sign-in state, time rules, language preferences, dated usage durations, break state and clock consistency information in a local SQLite database. It does not record browsing history, keystrokes, documents, camera or microphone input, or the contents of other apps. Passwords use PBKDF2-SHA256 with 600,000 iterations and individual random salts. The whole database is not encrypted; a person with sufficient Windows permissions can read or change local files.

Use, retention and sharing

Local data is used to authenticate accounts, apply time rules and show usage. Daily usage history is kept for up to 30 days. Accounts and settings remain until removed; upgrade backups and copies remain until you delete them. Kids PC Time Control does not upload family records to Top Shareware, sell personal information, or include advertising, analytics or cloud synchronization. Windows and Microsoft Store process data under their own policies. The recent 30-day window is pruned when the app rolls a record into a new day. If the app is not used, existing files can remain until the next use or manual removal.

Web links and support

Opening a website or contacting support sends connection information and any message you choose to provide to the relevant service. These static pages are hosted on GitHub Pages and add no analytics, advertising, forms or cookies. GitHub may process technical connection data under its own privacy statement. GitHub issues are public: never post passwords, a family database, children's identifying details or private screenshots. Support information you voluntarily provide is used to respond to your request.

Your controls

A parent can view recent usage, change rules and reset child passwords. To remove all local app data, first disable startup protection in the parent interface, sign out and close the app, then delete its Family data folder and any backups or copies. The normal location is %LOCALAPPDATA%\ScreenTimeControl\Family; packaged Windows apps may store it inside the package's per-user data container. This removes accounts and settings. Uninstalling may leave separately made copies. The publisher cannot remotely access, export or erase data that exists only on your PC.

Contact and changes

Use the support link on this page for privacy questions. Include only information needed to explain the request. Children should ask a parent to contact us. We update this policy when app data practices change and show the effective date below.

Trial and Microsoft Store purchases

The app also stores trial start and end times, observed computer time, purchase verification status and license cache expiry locally to determine feature availability. It queries Microsoft Store for this app and its full-version add-on licenses and opens the Store purchase dialog. Microsoft processes Store account, payment and transaction information under its policies. The app does not receive payment card information or send family accounts, time rules or usage records to Microsoft.
