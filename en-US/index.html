KIDS PC TIME CONTROL

Simple family computer time limits: daily plans, today's exceptions, break reminders and Windows screen locking, with local parent and child accounts.

Windows 11 24H2 or later · x64 PC · Local parent and child accounts

View Kids PC Time Control in Microsoft Store

Kids PC Time Control helps families agree on computer time on a shared Windows PC. Parents and children use the same clear interface with separate local accounts. No Kids PC Time Control online account is required.

Set a daily time budget, a maximum continuous session, a required break and periods when computer use is blocked, including overnight periods. Change the rules whenever needed. Today's temporary settings can replace the daily budget, session limit or blocked periods independently; they expire at local midnight without erasing time already used.

A child's timer starts on sign-in. A reminder appears before time runs out—three minutes by default, adjustable by a parent. When a limit or blocked period is reached, Kids PC Time Control requests Windows screen locking. It does not shut down the PC. Save work before the deadline. Parents can view recent daily usage and reset a child's password.

Optional startup protection asks for a Kids PC Time Control sign-in within 60 seconds after signing in to or unlocking the current Windows account. This feature depends on Windows allowing the startup task. It does not replace Windows sign-in, protect other Windows accounts or prevent an administrator from bypassing the app.

Family accounts, rules and usage records stay on this PC. Kids PC Time Control has no advertising, analytics or cloud synchronization. Supports English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, German, French, Spanish and Japanese. Requires Windows 11 version 24H2 or later on an x64 PC.

Free download. A parent can explicitly start one 7-day (168-hour) trial with all features, without payment or automatic charges. After the trial, unlock the full version permanently with a one-time in-app purchase through Microsoft Store. The planned US price is US$8.89; local pricing and taxes are shown at checkout. No subscription. When the trial ends without a valid purchase license, time control and sign-in protection pause while accounts and settings remain.

A Microsoft Store account is needed for purchases and restoration; no separate publisher account is required. Purchase licenses are checked periodically. After successful verification, the local offline license cache lasts up to 30 days; reconnect to the Store to verify again.
